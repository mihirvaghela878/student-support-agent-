# Illustrative Student Support Handbook

> Fictional example policy for this software assignment. It is not an official institutional policy.

## Fee payment issues
If the payment portal is unavailable on the fee due date, retain a screenshot or error reference and contact the Finance Support Desk. A payment attempted on a non-working day or during a verified portal outage may be completed by the next working day. The Finance Desk confirms whether late fees apply; this handbook does not guarantee a waiver.

## Fee receipts
Allow up to two working days for an online payment receipt to appear after a successful transaction. If it is still missing, submit a support request with the transaction reference. Never share a password, card number, CVV, or banking PIN in a support request.

## Scholarships
Scholarship applications are reviewed against the published eligibility criteria and available funds. An application marked under review is not an award. Students should contact the Scholarship Office for individual decisions and current deadlines. This sample handbook does not define a GPA threshold or guarantee eligibility.

## Course change requests
Students may request a course change during the first two teaching weeks, subject to seat availability and approval by the Programme Office. A request is not approved until written confirmation is received. Contact the Programme Office with the course codes and reason for the request.

## Support requests
Include the issue category, a brief description, and relevant non-sensitive reference numbers. Do not include credentials or full payment-card information. The support desk will provide a ticket number; use it when checking status. This illustrative policy does not specify a response-time commitment.
